Go live to play in AR

Start a livestream to YouTube from your drone's own app (DJI Fly, Autel Explorer, etc. — set it to Unlisted, not Public), then paste the link here. Dragunfly overlays nearby creatures right on your live video.

Positioning uses your phone's compass, not real computer vision on the footage — it's an approximation, not pixel-perfect tracking.

Fly Safe first

Dragunfly is a location game layered on top of your flight — it never controls your drone. You're always the one responsible for flying it legally and safely. Quick checklist before you take off:

  • Stay at or below 400ft above the ground — Dragunfly never places a target higher than that.
  • Keep your drone within unaided visual line of sight at all times.
  • Don't fly directly over people or moving vehicles.
  • Check your spot for airspace restrictions with the FAA's free B4UFLY tool before flying.
  • If your drone weighs more than 0.55lb (250g), register it with the FAA at FAADroneZone and make sure it broadcasts Remote ID.
  • Fly in daylight (or civil twilight with anti-collision lighting).
  • Never fly near airports, emergency response activity, or under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
