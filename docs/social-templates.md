## Launch post
> Pilots — we built something for you.
> Dragunfly turns your drone flights into a real-world scavenger hunt.
> Original sky creatures spawn at real GPS coordinates and altitudes near
> you. Fly your actual drone there, confirm the altitude your controller
> already shows you, and catch it. That's it — no drone hookup, no app
> store approval, no cost.
> First 500 pilots get a permanent Founder's Badge.
> Link in bio. #DragunflyGame #DronePilot #DroneLife

## Reply to "does this need my DJI/Autel app connected?"
> Nope — Dragunfly never touches your drone. It only uses your phone's
> GPS to check you're near the spot, and you tell it the altitude your
> own controller is already showing. That's the whole trick.
