# Dragunfly — Marketing Plan (zero budget)

## Positioning
"The first game you play with a real drone." Dragunfly is not another
simulator or FPV app — it's a scavenger hunt layered on top of flights
pilots are already doing.

## Target communities (organic posting, respect each community's
self-promo rules — lead with value/footage, not a pitch)
- r/drones, r/Multicopter, r/FPV
- DJI Forum, PhantomPilots, Autel Pilots Hub
- Facebook: "Drone Pilots Network", regional drone-pilot groups
- Discord: FPV Freestyle, drone racing league servers

## Content engine (post real capture footage)
- TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels: drone POV footage with the
  in-app creature overlay screen-recorded alongside it — "watch me catch
  a Legendary Auroralux at 380ft."
- Hashtags: #DragunflyGame #DronePilot #DroneLife
- Weekly "Catch of the Week" repost from the community, credited to the pilot.

## Influencer seeding (barter, not cash)
Reach out to micro drone YouTubers/TikTokers (5k–50k followers) offering
early access + an exclusive in-game cosmetic/badge in exchange for one
flight video featuring a catch. Cheap to scale because the "payment" is
in-game, not cash.

## Launch mechanics
- Founder's Badge for the first 500 players (see monetization.md) —
  gives people a reason to join *this week*, not eventually.
- Location-based launch: pick 3–5 known drone-friendly parks in your area,
  seed guaranteed rare spawns there for launch week, and personally invite
  local drone pilot groups to "the rare Auroralux nest at [park]."

## Landing page
`index.html` is the share-first entry point — built for link-in-bio use
on every social profile. It explains the "no drone hookup, no app store"
pitch in one screen, which doubles as the response to the most common
skeptical question pilots will ask.

## Metrics that matter early
Not downloads — first captures. A player who completes one real capture
is far more likely to post about it than one who only opened the map.
Instrument that funnel first.
