How it works

1. Open the map

Dragunfly shows creatures spawned at real GPS points and altitude bands near you, generated fresh every day.

2. Actually fly there

Use your own drone and your own controller. Fly to the marker, check your altitude readout — Dragunfly never talks to your drone.

3. Confirm the catch

Your phone confirms you're standing near the spot; you confirm the altitude your drone shows. Catch it, collect it, share it.

Install it like an app

iPhone

Open this site in Safari, tap the Share icon, then Add to Home Screen. You'll get a real icon that opens full-screen.

Android

Open this site in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

No store needed

It installs and updates instantly — no App Store or Google Play review to wait on.

Why it's free and permission-free

No drone SDK

We never connect to your drone. No DJI/Autel developer approval, no manufacturer partnership, nothing to install on your aircraft.

No app store

Dragunfly runs in your browser and installs straight to your homescreen. No Apple or Google review, no waiting, no fees.

No original IP

Every creature is an original Dragunfly design — built to be ours from day one.

Dragunfly is a location game, not a flight controller. You are always responsible for flying your drone legally and safely: stay under your local altitude ceiling (typically 400ft AGL), maintain visual line of sight, and respect no-fly zones and local regulations. Dragunfly caps every in-game altitude target at 400ft to match that rule.
